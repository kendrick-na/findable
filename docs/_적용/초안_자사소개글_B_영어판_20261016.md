**본문**

Findable (파인더블) is a GEO (generative engine optimization) service that measures how Korean brands appear in AI answers such as ChatGPT and Naver AI Briefing, and helps improve them. Its official site is findable.co.kr.

As more people ask AI instead of searching, it matters whether your brand shows up in AI answers and how it is described when it does. Findable measures this, finds what to fix, and helps you improve it.

## What does it measure
- Once you set a domain and questions, it checks how often your brand appears in answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Naver AI Briefing and other AI engines.
- It also shows which sources are cited and how your brand compares with competing brands.
- Based on the results, it points out what to fix first on your site and content.

## What it does not promise
- It does not promise that your brand will appear in any particular AI answer. AI answers vary by question and by time.
- Each result reflects the questions and engines used on the day of measurement. The conditions of each measurement are shown with the result.
